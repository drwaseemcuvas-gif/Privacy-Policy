# Privacy Policy — Journal Ranking System (JRS)

**Effective date:** 6 November 2026

Journal Ranking System ("the app", "JRS") is a free, unofficial tool developed by
Dr. Muhammad Waseem. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

## Summary
The app does not require an account, shows no ads, and contains no third‑party analytics,
advertising, or tracking. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information about
you. Almost everything happens on your phone.

## Information the app handles

**Searches by ISSN or journal title.** These run entirely on your phone, using a database
built into the app. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

**Paper lookups by DOI (the only feature that uses the internet).** When you paste a DOI or a
doi.org link, the app contacts Crossref (`api.crossref.org`) to find which journal the paper
belongs to. The request includes the DOI you entered and the app's contact email
(`zoolea3@gmail.com`), which Crossref asks applications to provide. Crossref may record the
request in its own logs under its own policies (see https://www.crossref.org/). No other
information is sent, and the app sends nothing to anyone else.

**Recent searches and favourites.** Your recent searches and the journals you mark as
favourites are stored only on your phone, so the app is useful offline. They are never sent
off the device. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app, removes them permanently.

## Permissions
The app requests only **Internet access**, used solely for the DOI lookup described above.
ISSN and title searches, recent searches, and favourites all work without internet.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone,
including children.

## Data the app does not collect
No name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers, usage analytics, or advertising data
are collected or transmitted by the app.

## Changes
